# New player guide

## Your Getting Started checklist

The app shows an 8-step checklist after you create your team:

1. Create your team
2. Choose a captain
3. Meet your squad
4. Prepare your team
5. Train a player
6. Strengthen your squad
7. Play a match
8. Start the Championship

Follow it in order. It covers the basics.

## Tips for a strong, balanced team

### 1. Know the Playing XI rules first
Your XI needs exactly 11 players, a captain from those 11, at least 1 wicket-keeper and at least 5 players who can bowl. Your league may also require a minimum number of home-country players and may cap legends. Details: [Playing XI](../01-team/playing-xi.md) and [Squad and league rules](../01-team/squad-and-league-rules.md).

### 2. Use Auto Build, then fine-tune
**Auto Build** picks the best valid XI and bowling order from your squad. Run it after every signing, then adjust by hand.

### 3. Check the Tactical Balance meter
The Playing XI page scores your XI against 12 balance criteria (for example a leg spinner, left-arm and right-arm pace, an off-spinner, a genuine all-rounder, left- and right-handed batters). Fix failing criteria before important matches.

### 4. Keep a real bowling attack
No bowler can bowl two overs in a row, and each bowler has an over quota (in a 10-over match, usually 2 overs). You need enough good bowlers. A red dot on the Bowling tab means your bowling order is incomplete.

### 5. Put batters in their zone
Top order is positions 1-4, middle order 4-8, lower order 8-11. A batter placed outside their preferred zone gets a -2 batting penalty.

### 6. Shop smart in the Signing Market
- Start from **Recommended**. It already considers your gaps, quota and budget.
- Tap the **XI icon** to see your Potential Playing XI. Empty slots are your real shopping list. Complete the XI before chasing upgrades.
- Squad fit matters more than money. Players weigh squad need, playing time, price and prestige. Paying 3x to 5x the base price barely helps.
- Don't forget **pool quotas**: you can only hold so many players from each quality tier. See [Pool quotas](../02-market/pool-quotas.md).
- Offers reserve your coins until the result, so keep some balance free.

### 7. Prepare before every league match
[Match Preparation](../03-matches/match-preparation.md) has 6 quick tasks worth +0.25 Focus Boost each, up to +1.5 on every player. Scout opponent and review fielding take seconds.

### 8. Match your XI to the pitch
- Dusty and slow pitches favour spinners and patient batters.
- Green and bouncy pitches favour pace.
- Flat pitches favour stroke-makers.
See [Stadiums and pitches](../04-tactics/stadiums-and-pitches.md).

### 9. Train players who actually play
Training adds +1 rating per level (up to level 5), but its effect wears off after matches and wears off faster for benched players. Spend on your regular starters. See [Training](../05-progression/training.md).

### 10. Think ahead to retention
At season end you can keep your core players. Build around reliable players, not one-season bargains. See [Retention and releases](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md).

## Common beginner mistakes

- Spending the whole balance on one or two stars and leaving holes in the XI.
- Forgetting to complete the bowling order before a match.
- Ignoring the pitch type.
- Letting a Signing Market win sit in the amber "Ready to Add" card on a full squad. It expires after 72 hours.
- Skipping Match Preparation.
