# What is Spin The Wicket?

Spin The Wicket (often shortened to **STW**) is a multiplayer cricket management game. You are the **manager** of a cricket club. You build a squad, pick your Playing XI, and take on other managers in leagues and tournaments.

The matches are played **ball by ball** with a **spin wheel**. Each spin lands on an outcome such as runs, a dot ball, a wicket, a wide or a no-ball. The odds of each outcome depend on how strong the batter and bowler are, which play style you choose, the pitch and the fielding setup. So every match mixes luck with the decisions you made before and during the game.

It runs in your phone or desktop browser as an installable web app, so there is nothing to download from an app store.

## What you do as a manager

- **Build a squad.** Sign free agents in the [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md), within your league's player-quality quotas ([pool quotas](../02-market/pool-quotas.md)).
- **Pick your Playing XI.** Choose 11 players, a batting order, a bowling order, a captain and a strategy ([Playing XI](../01-team/playing-xi.md)).
- **Play matches.** Quick Matches against bots for fast sessions, and league matches against other managers ([Quick Match](../03-matches/quick-match.md), [League Match](../03-matches/league-match.md)).
- **Prepare.** Boost your players for a match with [Match Preparation](../03-matches/match-preparation.md), set your [fielding](../04-tactics/field-setting.md), and check the [pitch](../04-tactics/stadiums-and-pitches.md).
- **Grow your team.** [Train players](../05-progression/training.md), earn [XP and levels](../05-progression/xp-and-levels.md), and chase titles in [tournaments and seasons](../05-progression/tournaments-and-seasons.md).
- **Keep your core.** At the end of a season you can keep key players through [retention](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md).

## Match format at a glance

- Matches are usually **10 overs** per side.
- **Powerplay** is overs 1-3, **Normal Play** is overs 4-10.
- Each bowler can bowl only a limited number of overs and never two overs in a row.
- Tied matches go to a **Super Over**.

See [Match formats and scoring](../03-matches/match-formats-and-scoring.md) for details.
