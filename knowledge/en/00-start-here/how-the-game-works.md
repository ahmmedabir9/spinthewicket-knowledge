# How the game works

This is the big picture. Each step links to a detailed guide.

## 1. Get in and join a league

- Sign in with Google, or choose to play without signing in.
- You are placed in a **league**. A league is a group of managers who compete together. League settings (squad size, starting balance, home-player rules, legend limits) are chosen by the league's managers, so they can differ between leagues. See [Squad and league rules](../01-team/squad-and-league-rules.md).
- In some leagues you request to join, a league admin approves, and you accept. Both approvals are needed before you are active.

## 2. Create your team

A short wizard asks you to:

1. Name your club and choose a short code.
2. Pick a badge.
3. Appoint a captain from the candidates offered.
4. Review and register.

After registering you see your starting squad, sorted by role. A **Getting Started** checklist then guides you through the first steps (see the [New player guide](new-player-guide.md)).

## 3. Build your squad

Use the [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md) to sign free agents. Your league limits how many players of each quality level you can hold ([pool quotas](../02-market/pool-quotas.md)). You can also [sell or release players, and trade with other teams](../02-market/transfers-and-selling.md).

## 4. Set your Playing XI

Choose 11 players, set the batting order and bowling order, pick a captain and a batting strategy. [Auto Build](../01-team/playing-xi.md) can do this for you. Also set your [fielding presets](../04-tactics/field-setting.md).

## 5. Play

- [Quick Match](../03-matches/quick-match.md) for a fast game against a bot.
- [League matches](../03-matches/league-match.md) against other managers, usually inside a tournament.
- Before a league match, complete [Match Preparation](../03-matches/match-preparation.md) for a Focus Boost.

## 6. Tournaments and seasons

League managers create tournaments (a season-long **Championship**, short **Series**, or **Knockout** brackets). You earn points in the table, and the Championship crowns the season champion. See [Tournaments and seasons](../05-progression/tournaments-and-seasons.md).

## 7. Grow

- Matches earn **performance points** and **XP**.
- Performance points are spent on [training](../05-progression/training.md) to boost player ratings.
- XP raises your team level ([XP and levels](../05-progression/xp-and-levels.md)).

## 8. End of season: retention and the next season

When a Championship ends, the league may start a **retention** stage. Your Playing XI is cleared and all contracts expire. You choose which players to keep, the rest are released as free agents, and you get a partial refund for them. Then the market reopens and a new season begins. See [Retention and releases](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md).
