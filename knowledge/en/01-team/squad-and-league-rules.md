# Squad and league rules

Each league is configured by its managers (admins). The same game can therefore feel different from league to league. **When a number below says "set by the league", check your own league's settings.**

## What a league controls

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Home country | Players from this country can get a **home boost** to their rating. |
| Home player boost | A bonus of 0 to +5 added to the effective rating of home-country players. |
| Minimum home players in the XI | Forces you to include a minimum number of home-country players (0 to 11). |
| Maximum legends in the XI | Limits how many legend players you can play at once. |
| Starting balance | The coins each team starts with. The usual default is 2,000,000. |
| Squad size cap | The maximum number of players a team can hold. Set by the league (the Signing Market guide refers to a 25-player squad cap). |
| One team per user | Many leagues allow only one team per manager. |

## Joining a league

1. You request to join, or an admin invites you.
2. The admin approves.
3. You accept.

Your membership becomes active only when both sides have agreed. Roles in a league are **owner**, **admin** and **member**.

## Rules for a valid Playing XI

A Playing XI is valid only if all of these are true:

1. Exactly **11 players**.
2. The **captain** is one of the 11.
3. At least **1 wicket-keeper**.
4. At least **5 players** with a bowling rating above 0.
5. It meets your league's **minimum home players** rule (if any).
6. It stays within your league's **legend limit** (if any).

If the XI is invalid, you cannot go into the match with it. **Auto Build** takes all of these rules into account when it picks your XI. More detail on building it: [Playing XI](playing-xi.md).

## Bowling limits

- Each bowler has a maximum number of overs, roughly the match length divided by 5 (so about 2 overs in a 10-over match).
- A bowler cannot bowl two overs in a row.
