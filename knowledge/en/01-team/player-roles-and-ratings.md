# Player roles and ratings

## Roles

Every player has one role:

- **Batsman**
- **Bowler**
- **All-rounder** (good at both)
- **Wicket-keeper**

When you swap a player in from the bench in the Playing XI, the new player must have the **same role** as the one leaving.

## Ratings

Ratings run from **0 to 100**.

- **Batting level** - how good the player is with the bat.
- **Bowling level** - how good the player is with the ball.
- **OVR** - the overall rating. It drives a player's price tier and which [quota pool](../02-market/pool-quotas.md) they belong to.

The highest-rated players in the app are in the mid-90s (the Players to Watch list showed ratings up to 96). Base ratings are set by the game team using real-world T20 form and are reviewed from time to time.

## Batting position zones

Each player has one or more preferred zones: **top order**, **middle order** or **lower order**. Batting outside the preferred zone costs **-2 batting**. See [Playing XI](playing-xi.md).

## Legends

Some players are **legends**. They are priced much higher, and your league may cap how many can play in the same XI.

## What changes a player's strength in a match

A player's effective rating starts from their base rating, then:

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| [Training](../05-progression/training.md) | +1 per training level, up to +5 (level 5). Wears off over time. |
| Home boost | 0 to +5 for players from the league's home country (set by the league). |
| [Pitch boost](../04-tactics/stadiums-and-pitches.md) | -2 to +3 depending on the pitch type and the player. |
| [Focus Boost](../03-matches/match-preparation.md) | Up to +1.5 for every player, for one league match. |
| Out of position | -2 batting if placed outside the preferred batting zone. |
| Batting strategy and play style | Changes the odds of big shots and wickets, not the rating itself. |

## Price

A player's base price depends on their OVR, how popular they are with fans, and their performance record. Legends and very high rated players (85+) cost far more. Pricing is shown in the Signing Market. See [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md).

## Stats and rankings

The Team tab has a Stats area with player rankings (All Time, Season and Awards). Player pages show Overview, Stats, Cards, Pitch and Actions tabs. The **Pitch** tab shows that player's bonus or penalty on each pitch type.
