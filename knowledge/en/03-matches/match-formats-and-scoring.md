# Match formats and scoring

## Length and phases

- A match is usually **10 overs per side** (the league sets this).
- **Powerplay:** overs 1-3. **Normal Play:** overs 4-10.
- The two phases matter for [fielding presets](../04-tactics/field-setting.md): in the Powerplay at most 2 fielders may stand in the outer circle, in Normal Play up to 5.

## Bowling rules

- A bowler cannot bowl two overs in a row.
- Each bowler has an over limit of about the match length divided by 5. In a 10-over match that is 2 overs.
- At the end of every over the batters swap ends automatically.

## Free hit

After a **no-ball**, the next ball is a **free hit**. On a free hit the normal dismissals (bowled, caught, LBW) do not count. Only a **run-out** can dismiss the batter.

## Ties and the Super Over

If both teams finish on the same score, a **Super Over** decides the match: one over each, played like a normal over. Each team nominates **3 batters and 1 bowler** from its Playing XI ahead of time (see [Playing XI](../01-team/playing-xi.md)). If you have not set a squad, the top 3 batters and the top bowler are picked automatically. If the Super Over is also level, the result can go to the team that hit more boundaries, or be declared a tie.

## Match types

Matches in a tournament are **group**, **qualifier**, **eliminator**, **semi-final** or **final** matches. **Quick Matches** are one-off games outside a tournament ([Quick Match](quick-match.md)).

## Tournament points

- Win = **2 points**
- Tie = **1 point**
- Loss = **0 points**

Teams on equal points are separated by **net run rate (NRR)**. See [Tournaments and seasons](../05-progression/tournaments-and-seasons.md).

## Performance points

Every player earns **performance points** from a match. Points are added to the player's total, and that total is what you spend on [training](../05-progression/training.md).

**Batting:** runs scored, plus 2 for each six, plus 1 for each four, plus 5 for reaching 50, plus 10 for reaching 100.

**Bowling:** 15 for each wicket, plus 5 for a 3-wicket haul, plus 10 for a 5-wicket haul, plus dot balls bowled, plus a bonus based on economy (17 minus the economy rate, so it becomes a penalty above 17).

**Result:** +5 for a win, -5 for a loss, +1 for a tie.

## After a match

When a competitive match finishes, the game updates team results, player career stats and achievements, tournament standings, and the wear on trained players ([Training](../05-progression/training.md)). You also get a result screen with Man of the Match, top run-scorer and top wicket-taker, then a reward sequence (cash/tokens, XP, a career card and league rank).
