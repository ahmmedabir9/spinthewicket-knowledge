# Tournaments and seasons

## Tournament types

League managers create tournaments from the League tab (Tournaments, then **New Tournament**). The tournament archive shows these types:

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Championship** | The season-long tournament among all teams (for example 16 teams, played in groups). It crowns the season champion. |
| **Bilateral Series** | Exactly 2 teams play back to back, up to 5 rounds. The team with the most wins takes the trophy. |
| **Tri-series** | A short series between 3 teams. |
| **Knockout Cup** | A single-elimination bracket. It needs 4, 8 or 16 teams. |

Setting up a tournament takes a four-step wizard: **choose the format, pick the trophy, enter the clubs, name it**. Every match in a tournament created this way is 10 overs. The full fixture list is drawn as soon as you create it, and **teams cannot be added or removed afterwards**. Managers whose clubs are entered get a notification and a popup when it starts. See the [tournament screens](../07-screens/league-and-tournament-screens.md) for pictures of each step.

## Fixtures

In a round-robin every team plays every other team. The number of rounds is set when the tournament is created, and home and away alternate between rounds. Teams can also be split into groups (for example Groups A to D).

## Points table

- Win = 2 points
- Tie = 1 point
- Loss = 0 points

Teams level on points are separated by net run rate. In group tables the top teams qualify for the next stage (the app shows the qualifying spots in the Points tab).

## Tournament screens

A tournament page has four tabs:

- **Home** - header, head to head, top performers, next matches.
- **Matches** - all matches grouped by round, with All / My matches and filters.
- **Points** - standings.
- **Stats** - tournament leaders.

When a tournament finishes you see a completion popup (your finish, record, player of the tournament and rewards in coins and XP), and the tournament page shows the winner and a **Tournament Awards** section (Best Player, Golden Ball, Golden Bat). Finished tournaments move to the **Archive**.

## Results and awards

A completed tournament records the winner, runner-up, **player of the tournament** and **team of the tournament** (the best XI). Championship winners earn +2,000 XP and runners-up +1,000 XP ([XP and levels](xp-and-levels.md)).

## Seasons and records

Each completed Championship is one **season**. The season record keeps the champion, final standings, team of the season and records such as highest score, best bowling, most runs and most wickets.

When a Championship ends, the league may start the [retention stage](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md) before the next season.
