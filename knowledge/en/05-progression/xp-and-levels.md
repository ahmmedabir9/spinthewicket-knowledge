# XP and levels

Your **team** and your **league** each have their own XP and level. Your personal account also has a level and XP, separate from your team.

## How to earn team XP

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Play a match | +300 |
| Win a match | +200 extra |
| Train a player | +150 |
| Sign a player from the market | +250 |
| Win a Championship | +2,000 |
| Finish as Championship runner-up | +1,000 |

The league also gains XP from matches played in it and from championship results.

## Levels

- The maximum level is **100**.
- Each level needs more XP than the one before, so early levels come quickly and later ones take longer.
- A big XP reward (such as a Championship win) can lift you several levels at once.
- You get a notification when you level up.

## Achievements

Achievements unlock for milestones such as a first win, a first century or a championship win. They fall into batting, bowling, all-round and team groups.

## What levels are for

Levels show your progress. Some players are restricted by a league level requirement, so a higher league level can make more players available. Check player details in the Signing Market.
