# Transfers and selling players

The **Market** tab has tiles for Signing, Retention, Transfer, Sell Players and Market Control.

## Selling (releasing) a player

Use **Sell Players** to release a player from your squad. A **10% fee** applies to the release. A released player becomes a free agent in the [Signing Market](signing-market.md).

Release a player if you need a free squad slot (for example when a signing is waiting in the amber "Ready to Add" card because your squad is full), or you want to clear your [pool quota](pool-quotas.md) for a better player.

## Transfer offers between teams

The **Transfer** tile (with **Players** and **Offers** tabs) lets you make an offer directly to another team's manager for one of their players.

- An offer has an amount of coins, and may include players as part of a swap.
- The other manager can **accept** or **reject**, and you can **cancel** your own pending offer.
- If accepted, the player moves to the new team, the coins are exchanged between the two balances, and both teams' overall ratings are updated.
- Transfers are not available during the retention stage.
