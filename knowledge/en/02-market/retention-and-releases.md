# Retention and releases

Retention is how you keep your core players from one season to the next.

## When it happens

After a **Championship** ends, the league can start a **retention stage**. Leagues can choose to run it every season or only every few seasons, so check your league. The Retention tile in the Market tab stays **locked until season end**.

## What happens when retention starts

- Your **Playing XI is cleared**.
- Every player on your roster has their **contract expired**.
- The [Signing Market](signing-market.md) is closed during this time. It closes automatically when the season moves into the finale or retention stage.

## What you do

Choose the players you want to **retain**. There is no fixed limit on how many you can keep, so the choice is yours. Think about quality, balance and your [pool quotas](pool-quotas.md).

## How it ends

The retention window lasts a number of days set by the league (between 1 and 30, with 1 as the usual default). It ends automatically when the time is up, or earlier if an admin ends it.

When it ends:

- Every player you did **not** retain is **released** and becomes a free agent.
- You receive a **refund** for each released player: their base price **minus a 10% fee**, added back to your balance.

## After retention

The league opens the market again, and teams sign free agents (including those just released) before the next tournament begins.

## Releasing players during the season

You can also release a player yourself from the **Sell Players** tile in the Market tab. A **10% fee** applies. See [Transfers and selling](transfers-and-selling.md).

## Tips

- Keep players who play regularly and who you have invested training in.
- Don't keep everyone. Released money comes back to your balance and gives you room to rebuild.
