# Pool quotas

## Why quotas exist

Instead of limiting how much money you can spend, STW limits **how many players of each quality level your team can hold**. This stops one team from collecting all the best players in the league.

## The five pools

All players are split into five pools by their effective OVR (base OVR plus any home boost):

| Pool | Quality |
|---|---|
| **A** | Highest rated |
| **B** | High |
| **C** | Medium-high |
| **D** | Medium |
| **E** | Lowest rated |

The rating cut-offs for each pool are calculated for **your league**, so a player in pool B in one league could be in a different pool in another.

A typical balanced squad ends up with about 1 player from pool A, 1 from B, 2 from C, 3 from D and the rest from E. This is a guide to the shape, not a purchase list.

## How your limit is worked out

For each pool:

1. The league divides the players in that pool evenly between all teams.
2. Any leftover places go to the **higher-ranked teams** first.
3. If a pool has fewer players than teams, every team is still allowed 1.

## What counts toward your quota

Your count in a pool = **players on your roster + your active offers** in that pool. You cannot stack offers you would be unable to fit.

Offers that would break a quota are blocked up front. In the Signing Market:

- The **Open** view hides players your quota cannot fit (this is the default). **All** shows everyone.
- The **shield icon** shows your quotas for pools A to E at a glance.
- Recommended picks already respect your quota.

## Tips

- Decide where your stars go early. Once pool A and B slots are full, you must improve through depth and training.
- Withdrawing an offer frees that quota slot again.
- If a player you want is greyed out or missing, check the shield icon first.
