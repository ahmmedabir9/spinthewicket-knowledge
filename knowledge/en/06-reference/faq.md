# Frequently asked questions

## Team and Playing XI

**Why can't I save my Playing XI or start a match?**
Your XI must have exactly 11 players, a captain from those 11, at least 1 wicket-keeper and at least 5 players who can bowl, and it must meet your league's home-player and legend rules. Your bowling order must also be complete: a red dot on the Bowling tab means it is not. See [Playing XI](../01-team/playing-xi.md).

**What does Auto Build do?**
It picks the best valid XI from your squad and sets the bowling order too, taking your league rules and chosen formation into account. See [Playing XI](../01-team/playing-xi.md).

**Why can't I swap this bench player in?**
A bench player can only replace a player with the same role (an all-rounder for an all-rounder, a bowler for a bowler).

**What is the -2 penalty on my batter?**
The batter is placed outside their preferred batting zone. Zones are top order (1-4), middle (4-8) and lower (8-11). Move them back to their zone. See [Player roles and ratings](../01-team/player-roles-and-ratings.md).

**What is Tactical Balance?**
A meter that checks your XI against 12 criteria, such as having a leg spinner, left-arm and right-arm pace, an off-spinner and an all-rounder. Tap it to see which are missing.

**How do I set the Super Over squad?**
Use the Super Over button on the Playing XI page and pick exactly 3 batters and 1 bowler from your XI. If you don't, the top 3 batters and top bowler are chosen automatically.

## Signing Market

**How do I sign a player?**
Open the Signing Market, find a player (start with Recommended), check the interest gauges, set a price at or above the base price and send the offer. See [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md).

**Does the highest offer always win?**
No. Players weigh squad need, playing time, price and prestige, and then a weighted draw picks among valid offers. A very high offer helps only a little; squad fit matters more.

**How long does a decision take?**
If nobody else wants the player, it's instant. If rivals do, a decision window of 5-20 minutes opens. Offers in the last 2 minutes extend the deadline slightly.

**Can I see rival offers?**
You see how many rivals there are, the deadline and the heat level, but never their amounts.

**Where did my coins go after I sent an offer?**
Your offer amount is reserved from your balance. It returns if you withdraw, lose, or the signing expires unclaimed.

**My offer was declined. Why?**
Either the offer was below the player's rejection floor, or every offer was below it and the player rejected everyone. They stay available in the market.

**My signing won't join my squad. What do I do?**
If your squad is full, it waits in an amber "Ready to Add" card at the top of the History tab for 72 hours. Release a player to make room, then tap Add or Replace. See [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md).

**Why can't I see or bid on some players?**
Your pool quota may be full for that quality tier. The Open view hides players your quota cannot fit. Switch to All, or check the shield icon. See [Pool quotas](../02-market/pool-quotas.md).

**Can I sell a player?**
Yes, with the Sell Players tile. A 10% fee applies. See [Transfers and selling](../02-market/transfers-and-selling.md).

## Matches

**What is Quick Match?**
A fast game against a bot opponent close to your team strength. You can change the opponent before starting. See [Quick Match](../03-matches/quick-match.md).

**How do I play The Global Cup?**
Open the mode picker from the Home screen's **Play** banner, swipe to **The Global Cup**, then use **Enter the Global Cup**. The mode card says it is for **8-32 clubs**, **cross-league** and available **any time**. Detailed Global Cup rules are not documented yet, so check the in-app screen or ask a moderator if you need exact entry, fixture or reward rules.

**Who presses the spin button?**
The batting team's manager spins. Tap Spin, or press the spacebar on desktop. Fast Play skips the wheel slowdown. See [League Match](../03-matches/league-match.md).

**What are Conservative, Standard and Attacking (or Defensive, Balanced, Aggressive)?**
Play styles. Conservative keeps wickets but scores slowly. Standard is balanced. Attacking/Aggressive gives more boundaries and more wickets. Use Attacking when chasing a big total and Conservative when protecting a small lead.

**What happens if I go offline in a match?**
A bot takes over your team and plays at a balanced level. See [League Match](../03-matches/league-match.md).

**What is a free hit?**
The ball after a no-ball. Only a run-out can dismiss the batter. See [Match formats and scoring](../03-matches/match-formats-and-scoring.md).

**What is Match Preparation?**
Six quick tasks before a league match. Each gives +0.25 Focus Boost to every player for that match, up to +1.5. See [Match Preparation](../03-matches/match-preparation.md).

## Tactics

**How do I pick a fielding preset?**
Use the matchup tables in [Field Setting](../04-tactics/field-setting.md): choose by bowler type and batter hand. Presets lock in when the match starts.

**Which pitch suits my team?**
Dusty and slow suit spin and patient batters, green and bouncy suit pace, flat suits stroke-makers. See [Stadiums and pitches](../04-tactics/stadiums-and-pitches.md).

## Progression

**How do I train a player?**
Open Team, then Training. You need enough performance points, coins, and a player whose career stats meet the level's threshold. See [Training](../05-progression/training.md).

**How do I increase a player's OVR?**
Training is the main documented way to raise a player's match strength: each batting or bowling training level adds **+1** to that skill, up to level 5, while the training lasts. Match Preparation, home boost and pitch fit can also change a player's effective strength for a match. Base OVR is set by the game team and may be reviewed from time to time; there is no documented button that permanently raises base OVR on demand. See [Player roles and ratings](../01-team/player-roles-and-ratings.md) and [Training](../05-progression/training.md).

**Why did my player lose a training level?**
Trained ability wears off every match, faster if the player is benched for 3 matches in a row or if their stats fall below the threshold. At 0 ability they lose a level.

**How do I earn XP?**
Playing and winning matches, training players, signing players and winning championships. See [XP and levels](../05-progression/xp-and-levels.md).

**What happens at the end of a season?**
The league may start retention: your XI is cleared, contracts expire, you choose who to keep, and the rest are released with a refund (base price minus 10%). See [Retention and releases](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md).

**Do player OVRs change automatically when a season ends?**
The documented season-end change is retention: your XI is cleared, contracts expire, and you choose who to keep. The knowledge base does not say that every player automatically gains or loses base OVR at season end. A player's match strength can still change through training, Focus Boost, home boost, pitch fit and position penalties, and base ratings may be reviewed by the game team from time to time.

**How do tournament points work?**
Win 2, tie 1, loss 0; net run rate breaks ties. See [Tournaments and seasons](../05-progression/tournaments-and-seasons.md).
