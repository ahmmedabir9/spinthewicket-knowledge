# App navigation: where to find things

The app is mobile-first. If a screen has changed since this page was written, trust the app and tell the support team so this page can be updated.

## Top bar and bottom navigation

- **Top bar** (most screens): back button, your club crest, name and level, your coin balance, chat, notifications bell, and the league badge. Tap the league badge to open the **League Hub**.
- **Bottom bar:** **Home, Team, League, Market, More**.
- On detail screens the bottom bar changes to section tabs. For example Market shows Market / Offers / History / Scout, and a tournament shows Home / Matches / Points / Stats.
- The Playing XI, Full Squad, Training Lab and Fielding screens have no bottom bar.

## Home

- **Play** tile: opens the mode picker (swipe through modes, starting with Quick Match).
- **Next Match** card: go to the match, prepare for the Focus Boost, or see all matches.
- Cards for recent signings, active series and your season stats.

## Team

Tiles for:

- **Playing XI** - [guide](../01-team/playing-xi.md)
- **Full Squad** - everyone you own, with role filters
- **Stats** - player rankings (All Time / Season / Awards)
- **Training** - [guide](../05-progression/training.md)
- **Fielding** - [guide](../04-tactics/field-setting.md)
- **Settings** - club controls

The main Team screen shows your XI on a pitch with panels for Bench, Formation, Tactics and Roles.

## Market

Tiles for **Signing**, **Retention** (locked until season end), **Transfer**, **Sell Players** and **Market Control**.

Inside Signing, the tabs are **Market**, **Offers**, **History** and **Scout**. See [Signing Market](../02-market/signing-market.md), [Retention](../02-market/retention-and-releases.md) and [Transfers and selling](../02-market/transfers-and-selling.md).

## League

League overview (level, teams, season, stage), Next Match card, Tournaments, and Players to Watch. League managers create tournaments here. See [Tournaments and seasons](../05-progression/tournaments-and-seasons.md).

## More

- **Competitions:** Championships, Series
- **Rankings and Records:** Team Rankings, Team Records, Player Stats, Player Rankings
- **Explore:** Players, Stadiums, Teams, Compare Players, Compare Teams

## First-time flow

Splash and sign-in (Continue with Google, or play without signing in), welcome to your league, a 4-step team wizard (name and code, badge, captain, review), your squad reveal, then the Getting Started checklist. See [New player guide](../00-start-here/new-player-guide.md).

## During a match

- **Before the match:** a pre-match hub shows both teams, a countdown, an overall-rating comparison, Match Preparation and the stadium.
- **Live match tabs:** Live, Scorecard, Graphs, History, Info. After the match a Summary tab appears.
- **After the match:** a result screen (Man of the Match, top run-scorer, top wicket-taker) and a reward sequence. See [League Match](../03-matches/league-match.md).

## Other places

- **Notifications** (bell icon): signings, results, match starts and tournament events, with a Clear all option.
- **League Hub** (league badge): league admin settings (for admins) and your account, sign-in methods and active sessions.
- **League chat:** a chat and members panel for your league.
