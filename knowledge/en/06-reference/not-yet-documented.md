# Not yet documented

These parts of the game exist, but this knowledge base does not have reliable details about them yet. For questions on these topics, say plainly that the details are not available here and point the player to the in-app help or a league admin or moderator.

## Game modes

- The mode picker on Home has six modes. Only two are identified: **Quick Match** (1 club, 10 overs, earns stars) and **The Global Cup** (8-32 clubs, cross-league, any time). The Global Cup entry rules, fixture rules, rewards and the other four modes are not documented here. See [Home screens](../07-screens/home-and-navigation.md).
- Other competitive or fantasy-style modes (for example score-based challenges) are not documented.

## Screens and features

- The **Training Lab** rules for tokens, training slots, timers and "finish now" costs. The screen itself is described in [Team and player screens](../07-screens/team-and-player-screens.md).
- The **Transfer** tile (limits, timing) beyond the basics in [Transfers and selling](../02-market/transfers-and-selling.md).
- **Market Control** and league **admin settings** screens.
- **Compare Teams**, **Team Records** and the **Stadiums** list. (The Compare tab in a player panel and the Players list are described in the screen pages.)
- The **Formation**, **Tactics** and **Roles** panels on the Team screen.
- How career card goals and rarity percentages are set. (What the Cards tab shows is described in [Team and player screens](../07-screens/team-and-player-screens.md).)
- **Settings** screens beyond account basics.

## Rules

- Exact formulas for ball outcome odds. The in-match **probability meter** shows the odds for the current ball.
- Exact base price formulas.
- Reward amounts for matches and tournaments (they are shown in the app after the match), and what the second reward icon on the match reward screen is.

When one of these gets a guide, remove it from this list.
